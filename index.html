My Approach

Design Process

A research-first, iterative process that puts users at the centre of every decision.

01
Discover
User interviews, competitive analysis, and stakeholder alignment to define the real problem.
02
Define
Synthesise findings into personas, journey maps, and a clear problem statement.
03
Ideate
Rapid sketching, wireframes, and information architecture to explore solution space.
04
Design
High-fidelity UI in Figma with a component-based design system for consistency and scale.
05
Test
Usability sessions, heuristic reviews, and iterating until the experience feels effortless.
